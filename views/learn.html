What is the Stock Market?

A marketplace where ownership in companies (shares) are issued and traded. Prices reflect supply/demand and expectations of future business performance.

What are Shares?

Shares represent partial ownership in a company. Shareholders participate in potential profits (dividends/capital gains) and risks.

What is SIP?

Systematic Investment Plan: investing a fixed amount at regular intervals to build discipline and average out purchase prices. It is a savings discipline, not a guarantee.

Trading vs Investing

Trading: short-term positions based on price moves; higher activity and risk. Investing: long-term ownership based on fundamentals; patience and research focused.

Risk Management Basics

Diversify across sectors, set allocation limits, avoid leverage, and review periodically. Never invest money needed for near-term essentials.
