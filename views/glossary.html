Investor Glossary

Alphabetical quick definitions. Educational only.

Asset Allocation

Distribution of investments across asset classes to balance risk and return.

Bear Market

A period of falling prices, typically a decline of 20% or more from recent highs.

Beta

Measures a stock’s sensitivity to market movements. Beta >1 = more volatile than market.

Compound Interest

Growth on both the initial principal and accumulated interest over time.

Diversification

Spreading investments to reduce the impact of any single asset’s performance.

Expense Ratio

Annual fee charged by a fund, expressed as a percentage of assets.

Index

A basket of securities representing a market segment (e.g., broad market index).

Liquidity

How quickly an asset can be converted to cash without significant price impact.

Market Capitalization

Company value calculated as share price × total shares outstanding.

Risk-Adjusted Return

Return measurement that factors in the level of risk taken to achieve it.

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)

Regular, fixed-amount investing approach to instill discipline.

Volatility

Statistical measure of price dispersion; higher volatility = larger price swings.
